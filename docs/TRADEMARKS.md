# GlideMix name and logo policy

*Draft, 2026-10-31. Not legal advice: have an IP lawyer check it before
publishing, and register the name before relying on it.*

GlideMix is free software: its source code is licensed under the GNU
General Public License, version 3 (see LICENSE.txt). You may study, change,
build and share it under that licence.

The licence covers the **code**. It does not cover the **GlideMix name, the
GlideMix logo and app icon, or the look of the glidemix.com website** (the
"GlideMix marks"). As section 7(e) of the GPLv3 allows, no rights to use the
GlideMix marks are granted with the code. This policy says what you may do
with them.

## Why

When someone downloads "GlideMix", they should get the app we made, tested
and support, from us. A version changed by someone else may be good, but it
is not ours, and people should be able to tell the difference.

## Always fine, no need to ask

- Saying true things about GlideMix: reviews, tutorials, articles, videos,
  comparisons, "works with GlideMix", "my mix made in GlideMix".
- Sharing the official, unchanged GlideMix downloads from
  github.com/cristitm76/glidemix-releases or glidemix.com, under their
  original name.
- Building GlideMix from source for **your own use**, under any name.
- Saying your own project is "based on GlideMix" or "a fork of GlideMix",
  as long as its own name and icon are clearly different and you don't
  suggest we made, checked or support it.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Releasing a changed version (a fork, a rebuild with changes, a "cracked"
  or "unlocked" build) **under the name GlideMix**, a confusingly similar
  name (e.g. "GlideMix Pro", "GlydeMix", "GlideMixer"), or with the GlideMix
  logo or icon.
- Using the GlideMix marks as part of your own product, company, app-store
  listing, domain name or social media account name.
- Suggesting that we endorse, sponsor, check or support your product or
  build.
- Changing the GlideMix logo or making new logos that look like it.

## If you make a fork

The GPL lets you, and we're happy to see what you build. Before you
release it:

1. Give it a new name and a new icon.
2. Remove the GlideMix logo, the app icon and the glidemix.com links from
   the app and its installer.
3. Point updates, crash reports and the licence check at your own servers,
   or switch them off. Don't use ours.
4. Keep the copyright notices and the GPL as the licence requires. Saying
   "based on GlideMix" is fine.
