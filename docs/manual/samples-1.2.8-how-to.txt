GLIDEMIX 1.2.8 - SAMPLES AND STEMS: HOW TO
==========================================

New in 1.2.8: your own samples and sound effects inside GlideMix, a sample
line under every song, and stems of any part of a song - saved as samples or
changed right in the mix.


1. THE SAMPLES TAB
------------------

The Samples tab sits next to Music library at the bottom of the window.

Add your samples
  - Click "Add sample folder…" and choose the folder that holds your samples
    and sound effects (for example your sample-pack drive).
  - GlideMix reads every audio file in it, in the background. A big drive
    (40,000 samples) takes a few minutes the first time and seconds after
    that. The status line counts your samples next to your songs.
  - The first folder level becomes the categories (Kicks, Loops, Risers,
    FX…), the second level the packs. Nothing is moved or renamed.
  - "Folders" lists the folders you added: remove one there, or scan all of
    them again after you added new packs. Removing a folder never deletes
    files.
  - A folder on a disk that is not plugged in keeps its samples; they show
    dimmed until the disk is back.

Find a sample
  - Click a category tile at the top, pick a pack, or type in Search.
  - Left and Right arrows switch between the categories.
  - Click a column header to sort (BPM, Key, Bars, Length, Date Added…).
    Drag a header to put the columns in your own order.
  - BPM, key, loop or one-shot and the number of bars are read from the file
    names, the way sample packs name them ("Top Loop 128 Am", "Kick 04").

Listen
  - Click the play button on a row, or double-click the row.
  - A loop plays at the tempo of your mix, over and over, at its own pitch.
    A one-shot plays once, at its own speed.
  - Click the small waveform of the selected sample to play it from there.
  - "Preview every sample you select": while it is on, Up and Down play each
    sample as you reach it. Space switches it on and off.
  - The Preview volume slider is the same one as in the Music library.
  - Right-click a sample: Show in Finder (Show in Explorer on Windows).


2. THE SAMPLE LINE UNDER A SONG
-------------------------------

Every song lane has a SAMPLES button (make the lane a bit taller if you don't
see it).

  - Press SAMPLES: a lane called "Sample line" opens right under the song.
    That is where the samples for this song go.
  - Press SAMPLES again, or the eye button on the sample line, to fold it
    away and bring it back. The samples on it keep playing when folded.
  - The sample line stays when you take its last sample off it.
  - It is saved with your project.


3. PUT SAMPLES IN THE MIX
-------------------------

  - Drag a sample from the Samples tab (or a file from Finder / Explorer)
    onto a song's sample line.
  - A LOOP lands on the nearest bar and follows the song tempo: a 128 BPM
    loop in a 124 BPM mix plays at 124, in time. A 70 BPM loop in a 140 mix
    plays half-time, at its own speed.
  - A ONE-SHOT (a hit, a riser, a vocal shout) lands exactly where you drop
    it and plays at its own speed.
  - Drop the same loop again on the same spot and it goes right after the
    first one, on the next bar - nothing already there gets cut. Drop
    several files at once and they line up one after another.
  - Samples go on sample lines only: a sample dropped on a song lane is
    refused, and the status line tells you to press SAMPLES first.
  - Right-click a sample on the sample line for its own menu: Follow song
    tempo on or off, rename, loop, split, reverse, pitch and speed, colour.
  - Samples never count as songs: not in the SONG counter, not in
    transitions, not in the tempo line.


4. SAVE PART OF A SONG AS A SAMPLE
----------------------------------

Grab 8 bars of a groove, a vocal line or a break from any song in your mix.

  1. Select part of one song on the timeline (drag across it).
  2. Right-click the selection: "Save as sample…".
  3. The save window opens with a name ready, for example
     "Song name 8 bars 128 Am" (bars, BPM and key, so the Samples tab
     knows them). Change it if you like.
  4. Choose the folder. The first time it is the Samples folder inside your
     GlideMix Projects folder; after that, the folder you used last time.
     Any folder works.
  5. Save. The sample is in the Samples tab at once, ready to drag onto any
     sample line.

The sample is the song's own audio, clean: at its own speed, without your
volume line or effects.


5. STEMS OF A SELECTION
-----------------------

You need the stems engine for this (Preferences > Stems > Install, once).

Right-click a selection inside one song. Two menus:

STEMS AS SAMPLES - save stems of the selected part as samples
  - Drums, Bass, Vocals or Other: saves that stem of the selected part.
    Example: the drums of 8 bars from one song, to loop under another song.
  - All four: saves the four stems at once; you choose the folder.
  - Names like "Song name drums 8 bars 128 Am". They show in the Samples tab
    straight away.

STEMS - change the selected part right in the mix
  - Remove drums / Remove bass / Remove vocals / Remove other: takes that
    stem out of the selected part only. The rest of the song stays as it
    was. Great for an acapella moment, a drum break or a breakdown without
    the vocal. Short fades at the edges, so there is no click.
  - Split into stems: the selected part moves onto four stem lanes under
    the song (Drums, Bass, Vocals, Other), and the song goes quiet there.
    Then mute, fade or move each stem on its own for that part.
  - Undo (Cmd+Z / Ctrl+Z) brings the song back exactly as it was.

How long does it take?
  GlideMix separates the whole song once, then cuts out your selection - it
  sounds better at the edges than separating a short piece. The first time
  takes about half a minute for a song (a progress window shows it, with
  Cancel). After that the song's stems are kept, so every other selection of
  the same song is instant.


6. GOOD TO KNOW
---------------

  - Your samples are only read, never changed. GlideMix keeps its own list
    of them next to your music library.
  - Samples you save from your songs are for your own mixes: respect the
    rights of the original music when you publish.
  - Questions or ideas: the GlideMix Discord.
