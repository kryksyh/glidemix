GLIDEMIX 1.2.9 - BEAT, SAMPLE RIGHT-CLICK, LIBRARY COLUMNS: HOW TO
==================================================================

New in 1.2.9: Beat, a step sequencer for your own one-shots that puts a
drum loop on a song's sample line; a sample line you can hide completely;
a right-click menu full of sample tools on every song and sample; and every
Lexicon column in the Music library.


1. OPEN THE BEAT WINDOW
-----------------------

  - Press SAMPLES on a song to open its sample line (see the 1.2.8 how-to).
  - Press BEAT on the sample line (under FX / M / S). The Beat window opens.
  - The beat you are working on stays while GlideMix runs: close the window
    and open it again, the rows and steps are still there.


2. BUILD A BEAT BY HAND
-----------------------

Rows
  - Drag a one-shot (a kick, a clap, a hat...) from the Samples tab onto
    "Drag a kick, a clap, a hat..." - it becomes the first row. Drag another
    one onto "Drag another one-shot here for a new row". Up to 8 rows.
  - Drag a new sound onto a row to swap its sound.
  - Click a row's name to hear its sound (and show its graph below).
  - The light on the left mutes the row; right-click it to solo.
  - The two small knobs are the row's volume and pan.

Steps
  - Click a step to set it, right-click to clear it. Drag across the steps
    to set or clear many at once.
  - Shift-click a step: accent (louder), soft, back to normal.
  - Bars: 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16. Longer beats scroll sideways (2 bars at a time),
    the scroll bar sits under the rows and follows playback.
  - Grid: 1/16 (16 steps a bar), x2 (32 steps, 1/32) or 3 (triplets, 24).
  - Swing: drag the swing slider (50 % straight, 54-58 % loose, 66 % triplet
    feel, 75 % the most).

Play
  - The play button loops the beat at the tempo of the mix. The light runs
    over the steps.
  - Tempo: "Mix" follows the mix under the playhead. Click it to give the
    beat its own BPM, then use - / + or type a number.


3. THE ROW TOOLS (right-click a row's name)
-------------------------------------------

  - Fill every step / 2 / 4 / 8 steps, Even out 3 / 5 / 7 hits a bar.
  - Fill in: hits at 1/1 .. 1/32, or on the offbeat between them.
  - Advanced fill: a number of hits over a number of steps, as evenly as they
    go (Euclidean: 3 in 8 is the tresillo) or every N steps; Rotate turns it.
    Fewer steps than the pattern and the row repeats on its own cycle
    (3 steps against 16 rolls over the bar).
  - Rotate left / right, Reverse, Invert, Randomize, Clear.
  - Copy steps / Paste steps (also into another beat), Clone row, Remove row.
  - Cut itself: a new hit stops the one still ringing (on by default).
  - Choke group: a hit stops the other rows of its group (a closed hat
    stops the open hat - put both in Group 1).
  - Swing mix 100 / 50 / 0 %: how much of the swing this row takes.
  - Attack, Release, Key shift: soften the start or the end of every hit,
    or tune the whole row in semitones.


4. THE GRAPH UNDER THE ROWS
---------------------------

  - Click a row's name, then pick what to draw: Velocity, Pitch, Pan, Shift
    (a hit a little late), Repeat (2-4 hits inside a step: rolls), Length,
    Reverse, FX: Distortion, FX: Filter, FX: Reverb.
  - Draw the values with the mouse. Right-drag draws a straight ramp,
    Alt-click resets a step.
  - The quick buttons 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, offbeat, clear and RND fill the row's
    steps.


5. BEAT STARTER
---------------

  - "Choose a genre": House, Deep house, Tech house, Techno, Afro house,
    Amapiano, Disco, UK garage, Breaks, Drum & bass, Dubstep, Hip-hop, Boom
    bap, Lo-fi, Trap, Drill, Reggaeton, Dembow. The rack fills itself with
    kick, clap or snare, hats, open hat and percussion from YOUR samples,
    with the genre's groove, swing and tempo.
  - Random: any genre's kit and tempo, with a groove of its own.
  - The dice on a row: another sample of the same kind. New sounds: new
    samples on every row that isn't locked. The lock keeps a row's sound.
  - New groove (row menu): new steps in the genre's style.
  - Tune to the song's key (row menu): tunes a pitched sound to the key of
    the song above the sample line (the sample's key comes from its name).
  - Save pattern keeps the beat in the genre menu under "Your patterns".
    They live in Projects/Samples/Beats/Patterns.


6. PUT THE BEAT ON THE SONG
---------------------------

  - Press "Put on sample line". The beat becomes ONE seamless loop on the
    bar under the playhead (after anything already there) and follows the
    song tempo like any loop.
  - The file is saved in Projects/Samples/Beats, named with its bars and
    BPM, and shows up in the Samples tab to use under other songs.
  - Right-click the beat on the sample line > "Edit beat..." opens it again.
    The button then says "Update beat" and replaces the loop in place.


7. HIDE THE SAMPLE LINE
-----------------------

  - The eye now sits inside the song's SAMPLES button. Click it to hide the
    sample line completely - nothing is left on screen - and again to bring
    it back. The samples keep playing while it is hidden.
  - Handy on small screens and with the FX sidebar open.


8. RIGHT-CLICK A SAMPLE ON THE TIMELINE
---------------------------------------

  - Show similar sounds: the Samples tab opens with the sounds most like
    this one first (same kind, key, tempo and length). Click "Similar to ...
    x" to see all samples again.
  - Add compatible sound: puts another sound of the same kind, in a key
    that fits the song, right after this one.
  - View pack: the Samples tab opens on this sample's pack.
  - Resample: saves the sample as you hear it trimmed into
    Samples > Resampled.
  - Send to Beat: adds the sound as a new row of the Beat window.
  - Create variations: four new sounds in Samples > Variations. A loop keeps
    its length (Reversed, Chopped, Stutter, Dark); a one-shot gets Reversed,
    Up 5, Down 7 and Dark.
  - Split into stems: the sample onto drum, bass, vocal and other lanes.
  - Show in Finder (Explorer on Windows) and Export audio... (a WAV).
  - Consolidate: joins the selected clips into one.
  - Disable: the clip stays where it is, faded, but plays and exports
    silent. Enable switches it back. With more clips selected, all of them
    switch together.


9. RIGHT-CLICK A SONG ON THE TIMELINE
-------------------------------------

  - Show in Finder, Export audio..., Split into stems (the whole song).
  - Add compatible sound: a loop in a key that mixes with the song goes on
    its sample line (the line is made if it isn't there yet), on the bar
    under the playhead.
  - Resample: the song as trimmed on the timeline becomes a sample.
  - Consolidate and Disable, as above.


10. NEW COLUMNS IN THE MUSIC LIBRARY
-----------------------------------

  - Right-click any column header. The less used columns are in three
    submenus so the list fits the screen:
      Credits and extras: Album Artist, Composer, Grouping, Remixer, Mix,
        Label, Producer, Lyricist, Extra 1, Extra 2.
      Danceability, popularity, happiness (Lexicon).
      File and dates on the drive: Bitrate, ReplayGain, Type, Location,
        Samplerate, Channels, Date Modified, File Modified (drive),
        File Created (drive), Size.
  - Remixer to Happiness and Date Modified come from Lexicon; songs from
    elsewhere leave them empty (Date Modified then shows the file's date).
  - File Modified, File Created and Size are read from the file on your
    drive. Every column sorts with a click on its header.
